Rain Barrel: Help

Stuck on a puzzle or found a bug? Answers to common questions are below. For anything else, leave a note here and say which phone you're using.

I'm stuck on a level. What can I do?

Tap Hint: it turns one channel the right way (or places a tray piece). Your first hint in every level is free, and every level you finish with 3 stars saves another hint in your hint jar. You can also play any other open level in the same garden and come back later.

How do I open the next garden?

Each garden opens once you've collected enough stars in the one before it. Replay levels to earn 3 stars: use no more water than needed, and solve it within par.

What are the blue splashes?

Leaks. A channel is pouring water onto nothing, which wastes 2 drops. Turn the pieces so the openings meet.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too, and it doesn't move to a new phone by itself.

What does the game collect?

No personal information. See the privacy page for the details.
