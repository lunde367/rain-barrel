Rain Barrel: Privacy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Rain Barrel is a puzzle game. It has no accounts and asks for no personal information. This page explains, in plain words, everything the game stores and sends.

Your progress stays on your device

Stars, saved hints and settings are kept only on your phone, tablet or browser. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data) deletes them.

Play statistics

The game does not send any information about how you play. If that ever changes, it will only be anonymous facts (like "level 7 finished with 3 stars"), you'll be able to turn it off in Settings, and this page will be updated first.

Ads

The phone apps offer optional video ads that you choose to watch for an extra hint. Nothing plays unless you tap the Hint button after your free and saved hints are used up, and ads never play between or after levels. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier to show and measure ads. Google's practices are described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can limit ad tracking in your phone's settings. The website version shows no ads.

Purchases

Any purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. The game never sees your payment details; it only learns whether the purchase was made.

Children

The game is made to be enjoyed by all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome on the support page.
